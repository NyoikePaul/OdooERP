ERP for the
African Market

Purpose-built modules that go beyond vanilla Odoo — localized for Kenya's business environment, regulations, and payment ecosystem.

📱
M-Pesa Payment Provider
Native STK Push, C2B, B2C integrations. Auto-reconcile payments directly in Odoo accounting with real-time confirmations.
🏠
Real Estate CRM
End-to-end property management — listings, leases, tenancy agreements, rent collection, and PDF agreement generation.
🇰🇪
Kenya Localization
KRA PIN, VAT (16%), WHT, and eTIMS-compliant invoicing. Chart of accounts aligned to Kenya accounting standards.
⚙️
Custom Workflows
Bespoke approval flows, bulk rent invoice wizards, and scheduled jobs tailored to Kenyan business processes.
🐳
Production Docker Setup
Hardened docker-compose configuration with Nginx reverse proxy, Let's Encrypt SSL, and one-command backup.
🔐
Security Hardened
Env-based secrets, role-based access control, Bandit security scanning, and Dependabot auto-updates.

Included Modules

A production-grade library of custom Odoo modules built for real Kenyan business needs.

01
M-Pesa Payment Acquirer
Full STK Push, paybill, and till number support with instant reconciliation and transaction logging.
02
M-Pesa Connector Core
Reusable Daraja 2.0 API mixin — access token, STK push, sandbox/live switching.
03
M-Pesa Transaction Log
Persistent audit trail — every Safaricom callback stored with receipt, amount, status.
04
Real Estate Management
Properties, units, tenants, leases, and automated rent invoicing with PDF agreements.
05
Bulk Rent Wizard
Generate all tenant rent invoices in one click — filterable by lease, date range, and property.
06
Kenya Tax Configuration
Pre-configured KRA taxes, VAT 16%, withholding tax schedules, and eTIMS integration.
